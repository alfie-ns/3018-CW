## 3. Exhaustive Topic Breakdown with Complete Quotation

### Topic: Autonomous Vehicles & The "Trolley Problem"

* **Context:** Used to illustrate moral dilemmas where no outcome is "good".
* **The Scenario:** A car must choose between hitting a jaywalker (wrongly crossing) or swerving to hit innocent bystanders.
* **The Lecturer's Twist:** He escalates it to numbers.
  * *Quote:* "Should I say I will crash a person innocent on the side of the street? Because there are three persons on a state of affecting one... instead of affecting three, I affect for example, one person only." ^^
* **The Legal Void:**
  * *Quote:* "We don't have prisons for algorithms." ^^
  * *Assessment Use:* In Task 2, Q5, discuss the lack of legal frameworks for POMDP decision-making in safety-critical tasks.

### Topic: The "Nanny Robot" (Trust & Autonomy)

* **The Question:** "Would you let your child... with a nanny robot?" ^^
* **The Consensus:** Supervision is required.
* **The Risk:** Hacking.
  * *Quote:* "What the robot is watching or saying... can be at any moment influenced by external... algorithms." ^^
* **Cultural Divergence:** Japan is more accepting of this than the West.

### Topic: Levels of Autonomy (SAE)

* **Definition:** The lecturer references the Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) levels.

  * *Level 3:* Automated but driver must be alert.
  * *Level 5:* No human prevention needed.
  * *Quote:* "But there are other machines that are operated by themselves and they don't need even human existence." ^^

### Topic: Algorithmic Bias

* **Definition:** AI training on datasets that do not represent the population.
* **Example:** Facial recognition failing on non-white faces.
  * *Quote:* "For example like favours young white men... it was having unintentionally majority of white people." ^^
* **The Solution:** "Try to balance your data when you are preparing." ^^
