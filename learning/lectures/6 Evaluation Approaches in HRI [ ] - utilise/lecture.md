## 5. Coursework Success Blueprint [ESSENTIAL SECTION]

### Task: Designing/Critiquing an Experiment

* **Lecture-Approved Method:**
  1. **Define Problem:** Find an "open question... left unanswered by previous researchers."
  2. **Formulate Hypothesis:** Define the Hypothesis and the Null Hypothesis.
  3. **Select Variables:** Identify Independent (what you change, e.g., robot speed) and Dependent (what you measure, e.g., human perception).
  4. **Choose Metrics:** Mix Objective (errors, time) and Subjective (Standardised scales like NASA-TLX).
  5. **Run Pilot:** "Make sure that your system work well."

### Task: Justifying Sample Size

* **The "Genio" Strategy:** If your coursework involves a proposed study, acknowledge the difficulty of recruitment.
* **Verbatim Defense:** "Considering the complexity of the setting or setup... equivalent to 20 or 30 or 40. Certainly we cannot say it's different... measured for a sample of people."
* **Trap to Avoid:** Do not claim a study of 4 people is "conclusive."

### Task: Bias Mitigation

* **Approach:** If using Within-Subjects (same people), you must mention "counterbalancing" or "randomising order" (implied by the discussion on bias).
* **Lecturer's Tip:** "We try always to mitigate this by preparing the persons involved... ask them to write... remarks."
